    Region

    A geographic area with three or more AZs. Data stays in the Region you choose unless you move it. Choose by compliance and data residency, latency to users, service availability and price.

    Availability Zone (AZ)

    One or more discrete data centers with independent power, cooling and networking. Spread resources across at least two AZs to survive a data center failure.

    Edge location

    Points of presence in major cities that cache content (CloudFront), answer DNS (Route 53) and absorb attacks (Shield), close to users.

    Local Zones and Wavelength

    Extensions of a Region placed in metro areas or inside 5G networks for single-digit millisecond latency.

    Shared responsibility model

    Who secures what?

    AWS is responsible for security of the cloud. You are responsible for security in the cloud. The line moves as services become more managed.

    Rule of thumb: your data, who can access it (IAM) and how it is encrypted are always your job.

    Pricing

    How you pay for compute

    Same instance, very different bill. Match the purchase option to how predictable the workload is.

    Pay-as-you-go basics

    • Inbound data transfer is free; outbound to the internet is charged per GB.
    • Transfer between AZs costs money; within one AZ on private IPs is usually free.
    • Stopped EC2 instances stop compute billing, but attached EBS volumes are still billed.

    Cost tools

    • Pricing Calculator: estimate before you build.
    • Budgets: alert when spend crosses a threshold.
    • Cost Explorer: analyze and forecast spend.
    • Tags: split the bill by team or project.

    Support plans

    • Basic: free, docs and forums.
    • Developer: business-hours email.
    • Business: 24/7, <1 h for production down.
    • Enterprise: Technical Account Manager, <15 min for business-critical down.
